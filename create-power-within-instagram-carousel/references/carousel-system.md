# Power Within Carousel System

## Content movement

Use one source idea per carousel and one information action per slide.

1. Tension: name what the reader may have misidentified.
2. Distinction: separate two experiences that often get confused.
3. Metaphor: make the emotional reality visible through a source-grounded image.
4. Practice: give one small action found in the episode.
5. Invitation: ask one answerable question and point to the podcast and coaching.

Do not force this sequence when the source has a stronger native movement, but document the reason for changing it.

## Copy hierarchy

- Canvas: `1080 × 1350`.
- Safe horizontal margin: `72 px`.
- Metadata: `19 px`, uppercase sans, loose tracking.
- Slides 1 and 5: `76 px`, all caps, editorial serif, `x=72`, first baseline near `y=190`.
- Slides 2–4: `64 px`, sentence case, editorial serif, `x=72`, first baseline near `y=190`.
- Body: `32 px`, sans serif, `x=72`.
- Question: about `40 px`, editorial serif.
- CTA: `17–20 px`, sans serif.

Keep the horizontal anchor and top baseline consistent. The two bookends match each other; the three middle slides match each other. Set manual line breaks so all-caps bookends remain readable without shrinking below `76 px`.

Preferred typography:

- Editorial serif: Iowan Old Style; Georgia as fallback.
- Navigation and body: Avenir Next; Arial as fallback.

## Palette

- Sage: `#7D8965`.
- Sand: `#E7D9C5`.
- Warm white: `#F5EEDF`.
- Ink: `#414735`.

Use no more than two brand colours plus one neutral on a slide. A carousel may shift the dominant colour across slides while remaining recognisably one system.

## Natural visual grammar

Use minimalist structures found in nature. Let the structure explain the idea.

- Growth rings: accumulated experience, an inner centre, evolution over time.
- Open arc: incomplete growth, space for becoming, authenticity revealed through an opening.
- Sudden gap or broken rhythm: silence, ending, absence.
- Two lines meeting around a shared centre: co-creation or belonging.
- River or path: movement between people, invitation, agency.
- Compass: self-set direction and internal authority.
- Breaking or forking path: inherited certainty giving way to choice.
- Wave: uncertainty that must be met rather than controlled.
- Breathing rhythm: returning to the body before choosing a response.

Use one dominant gesture per slide. Abstract it into line, contour, field, rhythm, or negative space. Avoid literal leaves, flower decoration, generic sunrise imagery, gradients, glows, and stock photography.

## Layout rhythm

- Preserve negative space.
- Use asymmetrical balance.
- Keep the type system stable while changing the composition.
- Do not place the same centred title block on every slide.
- Do not let artwork cross the reading path.
- Remove one unnecessary element after the first render.

## Default final slide

Keep the invitation warm and compact. Use two CTA columns when space allows:

- `POWER WITHIN PODCAST · EP [number]`
- `powerwithin.riverside.com`
- `1:1 DISCOVERY CALL WITH RAN`
- `calendly.com/randing-work/coaching-session`

Keep the visual CTA compact and the two destinations distinct. Reduce CTA type as needed so the Calendly URL remains readable without colliding with the podcast column.

## Caption

Write `80–150` words. Add context or emotional depth that the slides cannot hold. Do not restate every slide. Run a separate native-English pass and end the editorial section with one specific question.

Add this approved CTA block after the question:

```text
Listen to 心里有树 Power Within: https://powerwithin.riverside.com/

Book a free discovery call to explore 1:1 coaching with Ran: https://calendly.com/randing-work/coaching-session
```

Keep these two destinations separate. Do not replace them with a generic `link in bio` sentence unless the user asks. Add three to five hashtags after the CTA: one brand tag, two topic tags, and one audience or transition tag.

## LinkedIn reuse

Use the exact same carousel copy, page order, visual hierarchy, and CTA destinations on LinkedIn. Do not create a separate LinkedIn argument or add a corporate tone.

- Export the ordered pages as one portrait PDF document post.
- Write a `50–100` word wrapper caption.
- Let the document carry the idea; the caption only introduces it, asks the same core question, and provides the two links.
- Use no more than two hashtags.
- Keep the Chinese original for the show and episode names when they appear.

Approved LinkedIn wrapper pattern:

```text
[One natural sentence naming the tension.]

In EP[number] of 《心里有树》, we explore [one source-faithful sentence about the idea].

[One specific, answerable question?]

Listen to 心里有树 Power Within: https://powerwithin.riverside.com/

Book a free discovery call to explore 1:1 coaching with Ran: https://calendly.com/randing-work/coaching-session
```

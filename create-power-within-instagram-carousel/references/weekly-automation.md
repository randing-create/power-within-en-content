# Weekly Automation

Use Europe/Berlin time. Trigger the Codex automation once on Sunday. Tuesday and Friday publication must come from platform-native scheduled posts, not additional Codex triggers.

## Sunday at 09:00: finalize, approve, and schedule

1. Read the publishing tracker and select the next two unpublished episodes in strict numerical order.
2. Pull each episode from the Power Within NotebookLM source. Build separate Source Cards and never fill a source gap with a plausible idea.
3. Produce two complete, idea-led five-slide carousel packs with no public personal story by default.
4. Apply the approved visual hierarchy: `76 px` all-caps bookends on slides 1 and 5; `64 px` sentence case on slides 2–4; common left anchor.
5. Produce the Instagram PNG set, editable SVG set, contact sheet, and the same ordered pages as one LinkedIn PDF.
6. Write an `80–150` word Instagram caption and a `50–100` word LinkedIn wrapper. Use the approved podcast and discovery-call CTA block.
7. Present both finished packs as the week's final review candidates. Assign the first to Tuesday and the second to Friday.
8. Wait for explicit approval of the exact files. Do not schedule either post before approval.
9. After approval, schedule both approved packs during the Sunday workflow: the first for Tuesday at 09:00 and the second for Friday at 09:00, Europe/Berlin.

## Approval and scheduling gate

- The recurring automation has no Tuesday or Friday trigger.
- Tuesday uses the first pack finalized on Sunday. Friday uses the second.
- Publish only when the user explicitly approved the exact current captions and visual files after seeing them.
- Treat any edit after approval as a new draft that requires new approval.
- Use platform-native scheduling so the approved posts publish automatically at the reserved Tuesday and Friday times.
- Schedule the same ordered carousel on both platforms: PNG slides on Instagram and the matching PDF on LinkedIn.
- Invite `@powerwithin.xlys` as Instagram collaborator. Schedule LinkedIn from Ran's personal account.
- Never create a separate LinkedIn essay.
- If approval is not received, leave both packs unscheduled.
- If access, login, collaborator selection, scheduled-document support, assets, approval, or either platform is unavailable, do not improvise and do not schedule only one platform. Report the exact blocker.
- Record the scheduled date and time after both platforms accept both scheduled posts. Advance the official episode sequence only after publication succeeds on both platforms.

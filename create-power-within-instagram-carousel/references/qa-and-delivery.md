# QA and Delivery

## Editorial QA

- Confirm that every public claim appears in the Source Card.
- Confirm that direct quotation marks appear only around audio- or transcript-verified wording.
- Remove unconfirmed personal details.
- Check that the public copy remains idea-led unless the user asked for a story.
- Check that the practice comes from the episode.
- Check that the final slide asks one answerable question.
- Check that the caption deepens rather than duplicates the slides.
- Read the caption aloud and remove wording that feels translated, formal, or mechanically polished.
- Compare the native-English pass against the Source Card and confirm that no claim drifted.
- Confirm the podcast CTA uses `https://powerwithin.riverside.com/`.
- Confirm the discovery-call CTA uses `https://calendly.com/randing-work/coaching-session`.
- Remove rule-of-three lists, AI filler, slogans, and overused em dashes.

## Visual QA

Inspect each rendered slide at full size and in a contact sheet.

- Verify `1080 × 1350` output.
- Verify clear reading within roughly three seconds per slide.
- Verify slides 1 and 5 use matching `76 px` all-caps titles, while slides 2–4 use matching `64 px` sentence-case titles.
- Verify every title shares the same left anchor and that no all-caps bookend line clips or forces a smaller size.
- Verify no clipping, collision, awkward line break, or art over the text.
- Verify each slide uses no more than two brand colours plus one neutral.
- Verify natural elements carry meaning rather than decoration.
- Verify the carousel changes composition without losing its visual family.
- Verify CTA labels, episode number, and both URLs. The podcast and Ran's Discovery Call must remain separate destinations.

## Cross-platform QA

- Confirm that LinkedIn uses the same slide copy, order, and design as Instagram.
- Confirm that the LinkedIn PDF has one page per slide and preserves the `1080 × 1350` aspect ratio.
- Confirm that the Instagram caption is `80–150` words and the LinkedIn wrapper is `50–100` words.
- Confirm both captions use the approved two-line CTA wording for 心里有树 Power Within and Ran's free discovery call.
- Reject any separate LinkedIn essay, professionalized rewrite, or 220–350-word feed post unless the user explicitly reverses this decision.
- Keep the show and episode names in their original Chinese when they appear in English captions.

## Delivery pack

Include:

- `content-pack.md` with status, Source Card, quote status, content thesis, slide copy, caption, and art direction.
- Five numbered PNG files.
- Five matching SVG files.
- One ordered LinkedIn PDF containing the same five slides.
- `contact-sheet.png`.
- The carousel JSON configuration when the supplied renderer is used.
- A ZIP archive when the user asks for a shareable package.

Use descriptive filenames. Keep the source and rendered files together. State clearly that the work is a draft when approval is pending.

## Approval boundary

Do not schedule, publish, create a collaborator invitation, or trigger coaching outreach as part of production. First show the user the finished copy and visuals. Continue to publishing only after explicit approval and a separate request.

Within the recurring weekly workflow, approval applies only to the exact reviewed files and captions. Any later edit invalidates the prior approval.
